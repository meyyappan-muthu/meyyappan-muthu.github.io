# Profile Photo Requirements & Tips

Your profile photo is the first visual element visitors see. Make it count!

## 📸 Technical Requirements

### File Specifications
- **Filename**: `profile-photo.jpg` (exactly, case-sensitive)
- **Format**: JPG or PNG (JPG recommended for smaller file size)
- **Dimensions**: Minimum 400x400px, recommended 800x800px
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square)
- **File Size**: Under 500KB (compress if larger)
- **Color Space**: sRGB

### Quality Standards
- High resolution (not pixelated)
- Well-lit (natural lighting preferred)
- Sharp focus (not blurry)
- Professional composition
- Appropriate contrast

## 🎭 Professional Photo Guidelines

### What Makes a Great Portfolio Photo

✅ **Do:**
- Use a recent photo (within 1-2 years)
- Wear professional attire appropriate for your industry
- Choose a clean, uncluttered background
- Face the camera directly
- Maintain good posture
- Use natural, approachable expression
- Ensure your face is well-lit
- Show your face clearly (no sunglasses, hats)

❌ **Avoid:**
- Casual or vacation photos
- Group photos cropped down
- Low-resolution images
- Busy or distracting backgrounds
- Extreme filters or heavy editing
- Photos with other people visible
- Selfies with arm visible
- Photos in poor lighting

### Background Options

**Solid Colors** (Professional, Clean)
- Neutral: Gray, beige, off-white
- Bold: Navy blue, charcoal
- Modern: Soft gradients

**Natural Settings** (Approachable, Authentic)
- Office environment (blurred)
- Outdoor professional setting
- Modern workplace
- Ensure background doesn't compete with your face

**Virtual Backgrounds** (If using)
- Keep it subtle
- Avoid obviously fake backgrounds
- Ensure clean edge detection

## 🛠️ Preparing Your Photo

### Option 1: Use Existing Professional Photo
If you have a professional headshot:
1. Save as `profile-photo.jpg`
2. Resize to square (crop if needed)
3. Compress if over 500KB
4. Place in portfolio directory

### Option 2: Take a New Photo

**Equipment Needed:**
- Modern smartphone (iPhone 11+, recent Android)
- Good lighting (natural window light works great)
- Simple background
- Tripod or stable surface (optional)

**Steps:**
1. Find location with good natural light
2. Position yourself 3-4 feet from background
3. Set up camera at eye level
4. Use portrait mode if available
5. Take multiple shots with slight variations
6. Choose best option

### Option 3: Professional Photo Shoot
Consider hiring a professional photographer for:
- LinkedIn-quality headshots
- Multiple outfit/background options
- Professional editing and retouching
- Guidance on posing and expression

**Cost**: $100-$300 typically
**Duration**: 30-60 minutes
**Output**: 5-10 professionally edited photos

## 🖼️ Image Editing & Optimization

### Basic Editing (Free Tools)

**Online Tools:**
- [Remove.bg](https://remove.bg) - Remove background
- [Photopea](https://photopea.com) - Free Photoshop alternative
- [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app) - Image compression
- [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com) - Smart compression

**Mobile Apps:**
- Snapseed (iOS/Android) - Professional editing
- VSCO (iOS/Android) - Filters and adjustments
- Adobe Lightroom Mobile (iOS/Android) - Comprehensive editing

### Cropping to Square
1. Open image in editing tool
2. Select square crop (1:1 aspect ratio)
3. Position your face in center-upper area
4. Ensure adequate headroom above
5. Include shoulders in frame

### Optimizing File Size
If your photo is too large:
1. Resize to 800x800px max
2. Use JPG format with 80-85% quality
3. Use compression tool (TinyPNG, Squoosh)
4. Verify file is under 500KB

## 📏 Positioning & Framing Guide

```
┌─────────────────────┐
│                     │
│      [headroom]     │  ← 10-15% space above head
│                     │
│    ╱───────╲       │
│   │  FACE   │      │  ← Face centered, fills frame
│   │ (eyes)  │      │     Eyes at upper third line
│    ╲───────╱       │
│                     │
│    [shoulders]      │  ← Include shoulders
│                     │
└─────────────────────┘
```

### Rule of Thirds
- Eyes should be approximately 1/3 from top
- Face centered horizontally
- Include some shoulder/upper body

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Lighting
- **Natural light** is best (near window, not direct sun)
- **Soft, diffused light** avoids harsh shadows
- **Golden hour** (morning/evening) for warm tones
- Avoid overhead fluorescent lighting

### Expression
- **Slight smile** appears approachable
- **Eyes engaged** with camera
- **Relaxed shoulders** show confidence
- Practice in mirror first

### Clothing
- **Solid colors** over patterns
- **Professional attire** for your industry
- **Avoid white shirts** on white backgrounds
- Consider what complements your site's blue theme

## 📱 Quick Phone Photo Guide

Taking your own professional photo with a smartphone:

1. **Setup** (5 minutes)
   - Clean background (wall, bookshelf, office)
   - Position yourself 2-3 feet from background
   - Place phone on tripod or stack of books
   - Position camera at eye level

2. **Lighting** (Critical!)
   - Face a window with indirect light
   - Time: Morning or late afternoon
   - Avoid direct sunlight (too harsh)
   - Use lamp if window light isn't available

3. **Camera Settings**
   - Use portrait mode if available
   - Enable timer (10 seconds)
   - Set camera at eye level
   - Use back camera (better quality)

4. **Pose & Shoot**
   - Stand/sit up straight
   - Angle body slightly (not flat to camera)
   - Relax shoulders
   - Look directly at camera
   - Take 10-15 shots with slight variations

5. **Select & Edit**
   - Choose sharpest, most natural shot
   - Crop to square
   - Adjust brightness/contrast if needed
   - Don't over-edit
